# Visual direction

Fieldhouse Forge uses a warm, editorial Midwestern athletics language without copying a real school, supplier, or apparel company. Fraunces supplies the expressive vintage character; Source Sans 3 carries navigation, body copy, metadata, and forms. Aged cream, forge navy, brick red, maple brown, and warm gold echo fieldhouse materials without relying on licensed collegiate colors or marks.

Composition favors broad image bands, squared catalog panels, rule lines, scorecard-like numbering, and restrained paper grain. Photography is original AI-generated architectural imagery with no real institutions, people, signage, or logos.
